About the Project

The Community Intelligence Reporting Service (CIRS) is an independent civilian initiative. Our sole purpose is to collect legally obtained, publicly visible information relating to potential crime or safety concerns and relay it to the appropriate UK authorities.

CIRS has no law-enforcement powers, does not act as police or security, and does not conduct undercover operations or private surveillance. All observations are made from public places only and are reported through official channels such as the police or Crimestoppers.

How CIRS Operates

Members of CIRS follow a strict, non-confrontational approach:

  • Observe and, where appropriate, record events only from places where the public is allowed to be.
  • Collect open-source information (OSINT) available on public platforms.
  • Avoid any involvement, encouragement, or participation in criminal activity.
  • Report relevant information to official UK authorities for them to handle.

Our role is simple: observe, document, and report. The decisions and actions remain with the legal authorities.

What CIRS Is Not

  • Not a police force, security company, or government body.
  • Not authorised to detain, search, question, or arrest anyone.
  • Not permitted to enter private property or use hidden devices.
  • Not involved in undercover work, entrapment, or secret operations.
